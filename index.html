About

I am the founder of SentinelWeb, a security and cyber-risk assessment service for websites. I started with IT and network security (port scanning, vulnerability assessment, OWASP Top 10, server hardening) and now build complete web products end to end: backend, database, responsive and right-to-left front end, deployment with Docker and Nginx, and the security audit at the end.

I am currently taking the CompTIA Network+ technical and vocational course and hold several Kyokushin karate medals; both taught me the same thing, that results come from daily practice. My goals are to build products that are both attractive and secure, keep learning cybersecurity and software engineering, and grow SentinelWeb into a company businesses trust.
